Meet Your Instructor

Experienced.
Qualified.
Dedicated.


J.E. Cheng holds a Master's Degree in Music, specialising in Piano Pedagogy and Piano Performance, as well as the Licentiate Diploma in Piano Performance (LTCL) from Trinity College London with Distinction.

With more than 20 years of teaching experience, J.E. has taught students from as young as 5 years old to adults in their 60s.

He also has experience working with students with different learning needs, adapting his approach according to each student's ability, pace and goals.

“Good teaching is not about making every student learn the same way. It is about finding the way that works best for each student.”

Piano Programmes

Lessons shaped around the learner.

01 · All Ages

Basic One-to-One Piano Lessons

A strong foundation for students who are new to the piano.

Lessons cover piano technique, music reading, theory, repertoire and musical understanding.

Examination preparation for ABRSM or Trinity can be included according to the student's goals and readiness.

Children · Beginners · Returning Students
02 · Teenagers

Teen Piano — Focused & Accelerated

Designed for teenagers who have limited time or are starting later.

Having personally started piano at 14, J.E. understands the challenges faced by students who begin during their teenage years.

The programme focuses on meaningful progress without unnecessarily compromising the fundamentals.

Teenagers · Late Starters · Limited Time
03 · Adults

Adult Piano Lessons

Learning piano as an adult can be very different from learning as a child.

Whether you want to learn for leisure, pick up a new skill, return to music or pursue piano seriously, lessons can be personalised around you.

Beginners · Returning Players · Hobbyists · Serious Learners
